Brand Identity & Personality

Creative: Bold choices, unexpected accents, and a playground mentality.

Contrast: Extreme darks and lights paired with neon accents.

Expressive: Functional emojis guide user experience and add character.

⚡ Neon Accent System

Interactive highlights stay consistent in both light and dark themes:

Neon Electric Blue #7ECFFF Neon Fuchsia #F102CE Cyber Lime #39FF14 Radioactive Orange #FF5722 Acid Purple #A020F0
  • Blue: Primary links and action buttons.
  • Fuchsia: Framing, glow shadows, hover lift states.
  • Lime: Active statuses and successful runs.
  • Orange: Experimental or processing indicators.
  • Purple: Cutting-edge or backend-heavy lab projects.

🧪 Typography & Layout Rules

  • Font: 'Segoe UI', Arial, sans-serif.
  • Cards: Rounded corners (12px-16px), translucent layers, and glow edges.
  • Micro-animations: Fast hover feedback with transition: all 0.2s.
